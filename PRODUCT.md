# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Public site**: Activity coordinators ("animatoren" / "begeleidsters") in Flemish residential care homes (woonzorgcentra). Mostly women aged 35–55, practically oriented, not tech-native. They visit during work breaks or preparation time, looking for concrete activity ideas they can implement tomorrow. Job to be done: **find, adapt, and share practical activity ideas** that genuinely improve residents' quality of life.

**Admin section**: Used exclusively by the platform administrator (single person, technically proficient). Internal tools for platform health, content quality scores, and AI suggestion adoption.

## Product Purpose

Hartverwarmers is a Dutch-language platform for sharing elderly care initiatives. Coordinators share practical elaborations ("fiches") of activity initiatives, organized around the DIAMANT model — a 7-goal pedagogical framework. Success = coordinators finding usable ideas quickly, contributing their own, and feeling part of a peer community.

## Brand Personality

**Warm, Practical, Encouraging.** The platform speaks like a supportive colleague — never academic, never condescending. It celebrates the small, real moments of care work. Tone is direct and hopeful: "you can do this too." Primary emotion: **belonging and pride** — "I'm part of something meaningful." Visual tone: warm, tactile, handcrafted — like a well-loved scrapbook or bulletin board in a care home common room. Paper textures (polaroid frames, ruled-paper quotes, stacked fiche cards), cream and orange warmth. Light mode only.

All copy is Belgian Dutch (Flemish), imperative voice ("Registreer", "Ontdek", "Deel"). No Hollandisms. Flemish care vocabulary: "woonzorgcentrum", "bewoners", "begeleidster", "animatoren".

## Anti-references

- Clinical healthcare portals, cold SaaS dashboards, overly minimal tech aesthetics.
- Anything institutional or sterile — the platform is the opposite of the environment these users are trying to humanize.
- Dark mode; corporate polish over personal warmth.

## Design Principles

1. **Warmth over polish** — cream backgrounds, soft shadows, rounded corners, organic touches beat pixel-perfect minimalism.
2. **Content is the hero** — initiatives, fiches, and DIAMANT goals are the stars; UI chrome recedes.
3. **Encourage, don't overwhelm** — progressive disclosure, outcome-driven headings, celebrate contributions.
4. **Data honesty in admin** — show what's real; counts primary, rates secondary; "te weinig data" over misleading 100%s.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Good contrast on warm backgrounds, generous click targets, readable font sizes (base 17px for forms). Many users are 40–60 and not digitally fluent. Reduced-motion alternatives for celebratory animations.
